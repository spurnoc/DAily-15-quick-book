Privacy Policy

Daily 15 · Last updated: September 28, 2026

Daily 15 is a private reporting dashboard operated by SPUR for Yans Deli as part of the Dell Canada x SPUR "Trial #001" pilot. This policy explains what data Daily 15 uses, why, and how it's protected.

What we access

When you connect QuickBooks Online, Daily 15 reads accounting data from your QuickBooks company through Intuit's official API, such as sales, expenses, and invoice totals. Access is read-only. We never change or delete anything in your QuickBooks account, and we never see your QuickBooks password.

Why we use it

Only to show your own business metrics on your Daily 15 dashboard. We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't combine it with other businesses' data.

Who can see it

Authorized staff of your business and the SPUR project team working on your dashboard. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

How it's protected

  • Connections use Intuit's secure OAuth 2.0 sign-in, so you approve access on Intuit's own page.
  • Access tokens and app keys are kept as encrypted secrets and never placed in the public web page.
  • Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

How long we keep it

We keep data only as long as needed to run your dashboard during the pilot. When you disconnect or ask us to, we stop accessing your data and delete stored copies and tokens within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about your business, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also disconnect at any time from your QuickBooks settings under Apps. We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and let you know.
